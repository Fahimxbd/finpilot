# FinPilot disclaimer and use boundaries

FinPilot does not provide financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice. It is an educational automation toolkit that produces provisional summaries, classifications, calculations, and draft notes.

## Trading and market data

Indicator descriptions are based on historical data and cannot predict future returns. Labels such as “elevated RSI” or “MACD above signal” are descriptive, not recommendations. FinPilot does not execute orders and does not include brokerage write access.

## Accounting and tax

Transaction categories and anomaly flags require human review. They may be incomplete, incorrect, or inappropriate under a specific accounting framework or jurisdiction. FinPilot does not post journal entries, reconcile a live accounting system, calculate a final tax liability, submit a filing, or make a legal determination.

## Investment calculators

Calculator results depend entirely on user-provided assumptions. Constant rates are unrealistic; fees, taxes, inflation, sequence risk, volatility, default risk, and liquidity constraints can materially change actual outcomes.

## Research PDFs and AI

PDF extraction can omit tables, charts, footnotes, formatting, and scanned content. Extractive summaries do not validate evidence quality. Local language models can hallucinate. Review the original document and consult a licensed professional before acting.
